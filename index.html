Functional decompositions of even Euler polynomial pencils

Reflection about 1/2 gives a quadratic inner factor for every even Euler pencil. When can another nonlinear decomposition occur? The manuscript settles degrees twice an odd prime for rational parameters, proves a denominator obstruction in every even degree, and identifies all three exceptional parameters in degree eight.

Three results and their scope

  1. Twice an odd prime. For every odd prime p and rational c, all nonlinear decompositions of E2p(x) + c E2p−2(x) over a characteristic-zero field are equivalent, by an affine change of intermediate variable, to the decomposition through x² − x.
  2. Every even degree. If a nonintegral rational parameter in reduced form has a nonstandard decomposition, its denominator must equal 4, and the degree must be 2 modulo 4. These are necessary conditions, not a sufficiency claim.
  3. Degree eight. Over any characteristic-zero field, a nonstandard decomposition exists exactly when c³ − 6c² − 8c + 56 = 0. The three complex roots are real and irrational, so no rational parameter is exceptional.

The general even-degree conjecture of Pintér and Rakaczki remains unresolved by these results.

Lean verification

The log records Lean 4.34.1, kernel replay of 27 local modules, and 13 audited public declarations. The audited endpoints use only propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. Rational-parameter endpoints: EulerCanonical.twice_odd_prime, EulerCanonical.denominator, and EulerCanonical.degree_eight. The general-field octic criterion and root count are EulerEightExceptions.extra_iff and EulerEightExceptions.extra_count. Real isolating intervals are shown in the manuscript’s sign calculation.

The formal proof handles the four small primes using four points modulo 23. The manuscript’s single-point certificates are separately verified by EulerPaperCertificate.four_obstructions and can be reproduced by the included Python check.
